Last updated: 21.11.2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Roki Taxi handles personal data in connection with your use of our website at https://roki-taxi.si and when you order a ride from us.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes what information is collected when you send an order through our Website or contact us, how it is used, and your rights regarding your personal data. Please read it carefully before submitting an order.

3. How Our Ordering Process Works

  • The order form on our Website is provided by Google Forms and embedded in the page. When you submit it, your answers are sent to Google and stored in our Google account, where we can read them.
  • You can also order by phone, WhatsApp or Viber. In that case we receive the information you share with us through that channel, and the messaging service processes it under its own privacy policy.
  • Our Website itself does not run a database and does not store the data you enter.

4. What Personal Data We Collect

  • Identity and contact data: your name, phone number and, if you provide it, your email address.
  • Order data: pickup location, destination, date and time of travel, number of passengers, and any special requests or notes you include (e.g. luggage, child seats).
  • Technical data: when you visit the Website, your IP address and basic browser information are processed by our hosting provider and by the third-party services the page loads (see section 8). We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

We do not collect payment card details through the Website. Payment is made in the vehicle (cash or card) or by invoice to your company.

5. How We Use Your Personal Data

We use the information you send us solely to:

  • respond to your order;
  • confirm the availability, price and details of your ride;
  • arrange and provide the transport service you have requested;
  • contact you regarding your order (e.g. if the driver needs to reach you);
  • issue invoices and keep the accounting records required by law.

We do not use your data for marketing purposes.

6. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

  • Contract performance (Article 6(1)(b)): processing is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract (your order) and to perform that contract (providing the ride).
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)): where we issue an invoice, we must keep it for the period required by Slovenian tax and accounting law.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): we may process limited data to keep our systems secure, prevent fraud or misuse, and keep basic records needed to run our business. We have balanced these interests against your rights.

7. Data Retention

We keep order data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above:

  • Orders: until the ride is completed and any related questions or complaints are resolved.
  • Invoices: for the period required by tax and accounting law.

After that, we securely delete or anonymise the data.

8. Sharing Your Personal Data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We share it only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Drivers: we give the assigned driver your pickup details and contact information solely so they can carry out your ride.
  • Service providers: Google (Google Forms and Gmail) stores the orders and emails we receive. The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts, icons and scripts from Google Fonts, jsDelivr and cdnjs, which receive your IP address when you open the page. These providers may process data outside the EU under the safeguards required by the GDPR (such as standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework).
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose data if required by law, regulation, court order or a lawful request from a competent authority.

We do not share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

9. Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"): request deletion of your data where there is no compelling legal reason for us to keep it.
  • Right to restriction of processing: request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability: request transfer of your data to another organisation in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent: where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at roki.taxi.lj@gmail.com. We will respond within the time limits set by law (normally one month).
